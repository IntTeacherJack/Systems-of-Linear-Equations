The challenge

Lunch service orders

A school meal service prepared 52 lunches. Vegetarian lunches cost $4 and standard lunches cost $7. The total value was $292.

How many vegetarian and standard lunches were prepared?

1 · Model the situation

Two linked equations

Let v be vegetarian lunches and s be standard lunches.

v + s = 52 total lunches

4v + 7s = 292 total value

The first equation is easy to rearrange. We can write v in terms of s, then replace v in the second equation.

2 · Make a prediction

Which lunch was prepared more often?

The average lunch value is lower than $7. Which type do you expect there to be more of?

3 · Learn the method

Replace one variable

01

Rearrange

Make v the subject of the first equation.

v + s = 52v = 52 − s
02

Substitute

Replace v in the value equation with 52 − s.

4(52 − s) + 7s = 292208 − 4s + 7s = 2923s = 84, so s = 28
03

Find v

Put s = 28 into v = 52 − s.

v = 52 − 28v = 24, s = 28
