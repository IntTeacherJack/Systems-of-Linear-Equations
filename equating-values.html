Sustainable water access

Equating values

When two expressions equal the same value, set them equal to each other.

=
The challenge

Two water-access plans

A community organisation is comparing two ways to support a plastic-bottle-free water refill point. Plan A has a setup cost of €4 plus €3 per refill. Plan B has a setup cost of €12 plus €1 per refill.

After how many refills do the plans cost the same, and what is the cost?

1 · Model the situation

Two expressions for cost

Let x be the number of refills and y be the total cost in euros.

Plan A: y = 3x + 4 cost of Plan A

Plan B: y = x + 12 cost of Plan B

Both expressions equal y, the same total cost. At the point where the plans match, 3x + 4 must equal x + 12.

2 · Make a prediction

Which plan begins cheaper?

Before calculating, compare the starting fees. Which plan costs less when there are no refills?

3 · Learn the method

Set the values equal

01

Equate the expressions

Both expressions equal y, so set them equal to each other.

3x + 4 = x + 12
02

Solve for x

Subtract x from both sides, then subtract 4 from both sides.

2x + 4 = 122x = 8x = 4
03

Find y

Put x = 4 into either original expression.

y = 3(4) + 4y = 16

4 · Check your thinking

State the solution.

Give the number of refills and the cost when the plans are equal.
