The challenge

Choosing a city travel option

A visitor can use a bike-hire scheme or a bus pass. Bike hire costs $4 plus $2 per hour. The bus pass costs $10 plus $0.50 per hour.

After how many hours do the options cost the same?

1 · Model the situation

Two cost rules

Bike: y = 2x + 4 x = hours, y = cost in dollars

Bus: y = 0.5x + 10 x = hours, y = cost in dollars

On a graph, the point where these lines cross is called the intersection.

2 · Predict

Before graphing

Which option is cheaper for a short journey? Which one will be cheaper if the journey lasts much longer?

3 · Explore the graph

Where do the lines meet?

Select the lines to reveal them. Then identify the intersection point.

4 · Read the intersection

State the solution.

Give the hours and the cost at the point where the two options are equal.
